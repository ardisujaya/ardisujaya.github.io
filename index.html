A bit about my path

I started out watching over networks and systems in a Network Operations Center, learning to spot problems before they became incidents. That grew into hands-on System Administration, then into Site Reliability Engineering, where uptime and calm incident response became second nature.

Today, as a System Engineer, I focus on building and maintaining infrastructure that just works — combining operational discipline from my NOC roots with the reliability mindset of SRE.
